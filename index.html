03 / Principles

Isolation is a feature, not a constraint

AI agents are useful because they act. The same capability is the risk. We don't ban them, we give them walls.

01

Isolate by default

Agents get an ephemeral sandbox, not your laptop. Host escape and lateral movement across your network are both in the threat model.

02

Show your work

Every sandbox can be previewed as plain backend commands. Every prompt is a version-controlled file. Every output is reviewable before it lands.

03

Right-size the model

Most everyday tasks don't need a frontier model in someone else's data centre. Narrow tasks on small local models are cheaper and private.

04

Evidence over hype

Guidance grounded in documented incidents and emerging standards from NIST, OWASP and CSA, not in vendor promises.
